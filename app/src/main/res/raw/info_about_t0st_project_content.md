@t0stbrot.net is a open project about collecting cell data, the goal of the project is to map out most cell towers to be used in other apps or location services. All data is collected under the "Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported" license and free for anyone to download via the website.

The Contributor name is used in statistics and ranking as a unique identifier for your uploads, it can be named how you want alphanumerically up to 63 letters long.
